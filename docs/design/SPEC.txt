 Accessibility handoff
 Labels, roles and focus order for every new control. Hit targets are 44px or larger throughout.
 ELEMENT
 ARIA / ROLE
 FOCUS + ANNOUNCEMENTS
 Review sheet
 role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby="Bank activity counter"
 Focus moves to X close on open. Order: X → segmented filter → top card (merchant → amount edit → date → category → receipt → Add to record → Already recorded → Don't add → card X). Escape closes; focus returns to whatever opened it (pinned row, push, Books).
 Counter "3 of 7"
 aria-live="polite"
 Announces "Item 3 of 7" on advance. Not focusable.
 Card X (hide)
 aria-label="Hide for now"
 After hiding, focus moves to the next card's heading. Announce "Hidden. In Books under Unaccounted for."
 Edit amount button
 aria-label="Edit amount, $432.98"
 Opens the inline field and focuses the input (inputmode="decimal"). Done returns focus to the edit button.
 Category chip
 aria-haspopup="listbox" aria-expanded
 Chips are role="radio" inside role="radiogroup" aria-label="Category".
 Deposit options
 role="radiogroup" aria-label="What is this money?"
 Each option role="radio" aria-checked. Arrow keys move between them. Save has aria-disabled until a choice is complete.
 Swipe between cards
 Swipe plus "Previous item" / "Next item" accessibility actions
 Never swipe-only: actions exposed via accessibilityActions / aria-keyshortcuts (← →).
 Track toggle (Bank accounts)
 role="switch" aria-checked aria-label="Track Business Checking ••4821"
 Space/Enter toggles. Focus order follows the list. Start tracking has aria-disabled until one is on.
 Connect bank
 aria-label="Connect bank with Plaid"
 During handoff: aria-busy="true" on the page, status "Opening a secure connection" in a polite live region. Focus goes to Plaid's sheet and back to the bank card on success.
 Connect success
 role="status"
 Announces "Chase is connected. Choose accounts to track." Focus lands on the first toggle after the 1.2s sequence.
 Bank card status
 aria-live="polite" on status text
 Announces "Syncing" / "Up to date" / "Needs reconnect". The shimmer is aria-hidden.
 Bottom sheets (digest, disconnect, detail)
 role="dialog" aria-modal="true"
 Focus goes to the first option or primary button. Focus is trapped inside. Escape or backdrop tap closes, and focus returns to the trigger row.
 Digest time rows
 role="radio" in role="radiogroup" aria-label="Daily digest time"
 The checked row is aria-checked="true".
 Pinned "Unaccounted for" row
 role="button" aria-label="Unaccounted for, 4 items, $312.40, not in your totals. Review."
 Sits first in reading order after the page title. The label updates as the count changes.
 Brought in tile
 role="button" aria-label="Brought in, $15,071.00. Open Income."
 Push transition. Focus lands on the Income heading; back returns focus to the tile.
 Books tab red dot
 Tab aria-label becomes "Books, 3 items to review"
 The dot is aria-hidden; its meaning lives in the label. Same for Invoices: "Invoices, 1 overdue".
 Toasts
 role="status" aria-live="polite"
 Never steals focus. Stays 3s, or until dismissed while a screen reader is on.
 Match banner (Mark paid)
 role="region" aria-label="Matching deposit found"
 Inserted after the amount field in focus order. Link / Not now are buttons; after Link, announce "Linked to Chase deposit".
 Reduced motion
 prefers-reduced-motion
 Opacity only; no flight, spring or count-up. Values swap under a fade.
