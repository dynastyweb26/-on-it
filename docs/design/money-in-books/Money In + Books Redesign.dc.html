Chart encoding
Brought in: #6f9a78 area at 34%, solid 2.5px ridge #2f6b3f, circle marker. Spent: #c8412e area at 22%, dashed 2.25px ridge #b03a29, square marker. The expense layer draws on top, so the overlap reads as a darker band.
Not color alone: solid vs dashed ridges, circle vs square markers on the legend, peak labels and scrub dots, plus direct dollar labels at each peak.
Kept marker: a diamond per period under the axis. Gold filled = kept money. Hollow with an ink outline = spent more.
Text-safe colors (new tokens): green text #2f6b3f (6.6:1 on white), red text #a3341f (6.9:1). #6f9a78 and #c8412e are chart fills only.
Shape: cosine interpolation between periods, so there's no overshoot below zero and the crests round off like ridgelines. Y max = 118% of the tallest peak; three dashed money gridlines.

Swipe arbitration
1 · Edge first. A touch that starts within 24px of the left edge belongs to system back. The pair never sees it.
2 · Axis lock. Otherwise nothing moves until 8px of travel. If |dx| > |dy|, the pair captures the pointer; if not, it's a vertical scroll and stays one until the finger lifts.
3 · Inside the pair only. While this screen is pushed, the bottom-tab swipe deck is off for the whole screen. A horizontal drag only switches segments.
4 · Bounds. On Expenses pulling right, or on Income pulling left, the page rubber-bands: offset = 0.35 × (dx/W)^1.6 × W, at most ~6%. On release it springs home. Nothing leaks to a tab or to back.
5 · Commit. Past 30% of the width, or a flick over 0.5px/ms, it settles on the next segment (340ms, --oi-ease-out). Otherwise it settles back.
6 · Never captured: drags that start on inputs, buttons, an open sheet or the scrub chart.
7 · State. Both segments stay mounted, so each keeps its own scroll, search text and Week/Month. Tapping a segment runs the same 340ms slide.
