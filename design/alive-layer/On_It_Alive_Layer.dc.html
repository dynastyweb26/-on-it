On It. · design spec · Nov 2026
The alive layer.
Toasts, event cards, payment moments, revise-in-place and the command sheet, built on top of the screens you sent. The assistant is the one telling you what happened, in its own lane, every time.
01 Toasts02 Copy / share03 Card fixes04 Payments05 Record from chat06 Revise in place07 Event cards08 ? reply and empty state09 System
Assumptions
Matched from your screenshots: cream #FCF7F0, gold #D4AF37, ink #1E1A15, Montserrat 800 headings, DM Sans body, mono for doc numbers. The icons are Tabler, the same family the app uses.
Gold stays in the app, red stays on client pages. The client estimate and pay pages keep the red CTA. That includes the "Updated" tag and the "Signed again" state.
The example data comes from your account: Ruth Lawrence's E-0049 converts to INV-0032 ($50.00, 50% deposit).
Two directions for toasts and celebrations: 1a/1c Subtle and 1b/1d Expressive. Every other screen works with either.
Animations loop so you can read the motion. Live controls: Copy link, Share, the record-payment card, the "What I can do" reply, empty-state prompts and Convert to invoice. The chat header is unchanged.
01
Toast banner
