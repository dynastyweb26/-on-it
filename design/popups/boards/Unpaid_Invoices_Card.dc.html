HANDOFF NOTES
• All strings render as received. Only computed value: N in “and N more” = totalCount − rows.length (shown when > 0). Rows capped at 20.
• Row = one button (min 66px tall): client 17/600, “number · Due date” 13px, balance right. Overdue gets the existing calm chip (red dot + “Overdue”); sent gets nothing. Tap calls onOpen(row).
• [data-slot=row-action] sits right of each row, 0px wide today. Toggle “Show action slot” in Tweaks to see its 64px footprint.
• Thumbs: 44px targets, #8f8676 until used, filled on #f2e5b9 when selected. Same thumb again clears it. Down opens optional reasons (single-select, changeable). × closes without picking; a picked reason stays as a quiet tag that reopens them.
• Retry: disabled plus an in-flight guard from the first tap until the request settles. No double-sends.
• Scrolling: a new answer scrolls so its top shows (or all of it, if it fits). Opening the reason chips scrolls just enough to show them.
• Laptop (≥1280): same component with wide=true. Thread column 760px, card max 640px.
