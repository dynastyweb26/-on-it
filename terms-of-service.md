## 4. Getting paid

On It helps you show your customers how to pay you. **The money always goes directly from your customer to you.** On It never holds, receives, transfers, or has access to it, and we take no fee on it.

### Card and Cash App Pay through Stripe (optional)

You can connect your own Stripe account so customers can pay your invoices by card or Cash App Pay from the invoice's payment page. Connecting is optional, and you can turn card payments off in Settings at any time.

- **Stripe processes these payments, not us.** When you connect, you open or link an account directly with Stripe and agree to Stripe's own terms, including the Stripe Services Agreement (https://stripe.com/legal/ssa). Payments go directly into your Stripe account.
- Stripe charges its own processing fees to your Stripe account. Your On It subscription is separate.
- **Refunds, disputes, and chargebacks are between you, your customer, and Stripe.** You handle them in your Stripe account. We cannot issue refunds, reverse payments, or resolve disputes.
- When Stripe tells us a payment succeeded, we record it on the invoice. When Stripe reports a refund, dispute withdrawal, or dispute reinstatement, we add it to your ledger automatically. A refund lowers your recorded income but leaves the invoice paid; a dispute withdrawal lowers what counts as paid and can reopen the invoice. Check your Stripe account for the full picture.
- You can disconnect On It from your Stripe account through Stripe. Payments already recorded stay in your records.

### Payment handles (Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, Cash App)

You can add your own payment handles so they show on your payment pages. On It does not connect to those services. Those services' own terms apply to any payment made through them.

### Bank transfers

You can add your bank details so customers can send you a bank transfer. By adding them, you agree that:

- **The account is yours** (or your business's), and you are authorized to receive payments into it
- **You are responsible for the accuracy of the details you enter.** On It does not verify bank details. A wrong digit can send a customer's money to the wrong account, and we cannot recover it
- **Your bank details, including your full account number, will be shown to anyone who has the link to the payment page of an invoice you sent that is still unpaid.** They are not printed on your PDFs and stop showing once an invoice is paid in full
- **On It does not start, track, confirm, or reverse bank transfers.** You confirm with your bank that money arrived before marking an invoice paid
- You will **share invoice links only with the people you are billing,** watch your bank account for unauthorized activity, and talk to your bank right away about anything suspicious
- Your bank's terms govern your account, any transfer, and any fraud or error claim

### Payments you record

When you mark an invoice paid or partly paid, On It records what you tell it. We do not verify payments you record manually, and On It will not record a manual payment larger than the unpaid balance.
